Our Story

"I came to buy a house, and I bought a resturant!"

LA famiglia began as three brothers getting into the world of resturants just like their father. Keeping that classic home cooked feel while aslo creating five star quality meals. All of our food is family style feeding whole families just like how our father would.

We cook the way our father did: Everything made to order, treated properly, served without ceremony. Everything comes from our kitchen freshly made without question.

Catering & Private Parties
La Famiglia specializes in family-style parties. All food is individually cooked to order for you and your guests, and a private dining room is available.
We welcome engagement parties, rehearsal dinners, showers, communions, confirmations, special birthdays, sweet 16 parties, small wedding receptions, family reunions, and corporate parties.
